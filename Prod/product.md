# Product: Nasir Youth Community

A static, frontend-only community news website for the Nasir Youth Community. It publishes local stories, investigations, and a documented timeline of the Nasir Crisis (2025–2026) with source-linked reporting.

## Core Sections
- Home — featured stories, latest updates, Nasir Crisis timeline
- Newsroom — filterable story listings (News, Investigations, Community, Youth, Education)
- Nasir Timeline — searchable/filterable chronological record of reported events with verification badges
- About — mission, values, editorial approach
- Submit — community tip submission form (frontend-only; no backend configured)

## Editorial Principles
- All content attributed to named sources; no invented facts
- Casualty figures and allegations attributed to the reporting source
- Incident dates and publication dates distinguished wherever known
- Verification status shown per story: Reported, UN-documented, Corroborated, Details disputed
- Editorial notes included to prevent misattribution (e.g., multi-county figures not attributed to Nasir alone)
